## What happened

Several walk routes failed: stage steps too tall, a stair head bumping a ceiling.

## Why

The player can step up at most 18 units and is 72 units tall; the walker models exactly that.

## Rule

Stairs: risers of 16 or less. Leave 72+ units of headroom over every stair tread, including the last one under a floor above. Add a route for every new walkable area.
