## What happened

Real gamer handles and first names were baked into textures and READMEs; the history had to be rewritten before the repo went public.

## Why

The repo is public and every texture, commit and release zip is permanent.

## Rule

Never put a real person's name, handle, callsign or likeness in anything. 'Justin Con' is the one approved name. Invent everything else.
